SURVIVAL · INVENTORY

🎒 Gear & Crafting

Mine ores and ancient debris, smelt Netherite scraps, upgrade your gear, and collect one-time treasures from generated structures.

Page 1
Select an item stack

Survival hotbar slots start empty. Items you collect fill them automatically. Select a stack, then tap Equip / Hotbar to put it in your highlighted slot. Armor can be equipped. Drag item stacks to reorder them.

🪓 Inventory Crafting · 2 × 2

Select an ingredient and tap a square to add one. Turn on Remove to subtract. Place a workbench for the 3 × 3 grid, then tap the output to craft.

🐴 Rideable mounts & vehicles

Approach a horse, donkey, mule, camel, llama, pig, strider, or other mount and press Ride (M on keyboard). Some require a saddle or harness. Craft a boat, raft or minecart below and deploy it into the world. Shift or Ride again to dismount.
