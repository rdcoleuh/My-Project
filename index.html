📱 Put Minecraft Web on your iPhone Home Screen

In Safari, open this website, tap the page menu or Share icon, select Add to Home Screen, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add. The Minecraft Web icon will appear on your Home Screen. The game still needs an internet connection.
